FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about MockAI. Can't find your answer? Chat with us.

How realistic are the AI mock interviews?
+
Our AI is trained on thousands of real interview transcripts from top companies including FAANG, consulting firms, and startups. The questions, follow-up probes, and evaluation criteria closely mirror what you'll face in actual interviews. Many users report that MockAI interviews feel more challenging and comprehensive than real ones — which is exactly the point.
What types of interviews does MockAI support?
+
MockAI covers HR/behavioral interviews, technical interviews (DSA, system design, coding), case interviews for consulting, and role-specific interviews for Product Management, Data Science, DevOps, UI/UX, and more. We support 200+ job roles across tech, finance, consulting, and operations sectors.
How does the feedback system work?
+
After each answer, our AI evaluates multiple dimensions: content quality and relevance, communication clarity and structure, confidence indicators, technical accuracy, and use of frameworks like STAR. You receive a detailed breakdown with a score per dimension, specific improvement suggestions, model answer examples, and a comparison against successful candidates for that role.
Is there a free plan? What are its limitations?
+
Yes! Our Free plan gives you 5 AI mock interviews per month, one resume analysis, basic feedback reports, and access to our HR question bank with 50 questions. No credit card required. When you're ready to unlock unlimited interviews, the technical simulator, advanced analytics, and priority support, you can upgrade to Pro for ₹499/month with a 7-day free trial.
What is the placement guarantee on the Premium plan?
+
Our Premium Placement plan includes a job offer guarantee: if you complete all program requirements (minimum 20 mock interviews, resume optimization, and mentor sessions) and don't receive a job offer within 90 days of program completion, we will refund 100% of your subscription fees. Terms and eligibility criteria apply — chat with our placement team for full details.
Can I practice for specific companies like Google or Amazon?
+
Absolutely. Pro and Premium users can select company-specific interview tracks. Each track includes questions sourced from verified interview reports for that company, the company's evaluation rubric and cultural values, difficulty calibration specific to that company's known bar, and insider tips from engineers who've passed those exact interviews.
How does the Resume Analysis feature work?
+
Upload your resume as PDF or DOCX and our AI runs it through a multi-layer analysis: ATS compatibility scoring for 50+ applicant tracking systems, keyword gap analysis based on your target roles, quantification suggestions to strengthen impact statements, formatting and readability scoring, and a rewritten version with all suggested improvements applied. Most users see a significant increase in callback rates within 30 days.
